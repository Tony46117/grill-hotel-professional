About the House

Home / About

Since 2008

From one charcoal pit to the city's finest

Mean Grill began as a roadside grill with four tables and a single pit. The fire never went out — it grew.

Founder Chef Kamau Maina spent his youth learning from coastal fish-smokers and upcountry nyama choma masters. He brought that craft to Riverside Drive with one rule: nothing leaves the kitchen unless the fire made it better.

Today the restaurant spans a 40-seat grill room, a rooftop raw bar and a wine cellar of 600 labels — yet every steak is still hand-seared over oak coals in full view of our guests.
