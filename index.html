The House

A legacy built on coals and care

Since 2008, Mean Grill has been the quiet standard for serious grilling in Nairobi.

From the hand-built charcoal pits in our open kitchen to the sommelier-led wine cellar, every detail is deliberate. Our chefs train for years before they're trusted with the fire — and it shows on every plate.
